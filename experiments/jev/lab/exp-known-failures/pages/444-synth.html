Section 1: Dismissal of claims

(1) This section applies where, in proceedings on a claim for damages in respect of personal injury, the court finds that the claimant is entitled to damages in respect of the claim.

(2) The court must dismiss the primary claim, unless it is satisfied that the claimant would suffer substantial injustice if the claim were dismissed.

(3) In this section—

  • “personal injury” includes any disease and any other impairment of a person's physical or mental condition;

  • “related claim” means a claim for damages made by a person other than the person who made the primary claim.
